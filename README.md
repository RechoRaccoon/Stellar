# Install on Android
Download and run Stellar.apk from the [latest releases](https://github.com/RechoRaccoon/Stellar/releases)

# Install on Windows
- Download **Stellar-Windows.exe** (or **Stellar-Windows.msi**) from the [latest release](https://github.com/RechoRaccoon/Stellar/releases)
- Open it and follow the installer. If Windows SmartScreen says it "protected your PC", click **More info** → **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).
- On the last page, leave **Launch Stellar** ticked to open it right away. After that, Stellar is in the Start menu (and on your desktop, if you kept that option).
- **Updating:** download the newest **Stellar-Windows.exe** and run it — it replaces the old version and keeps your sign-in and settings. (Use the .exe for updates; the .msi can't update an existing install yet.)
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) is needed.

# Install on macOS
- Download the .dmg for your Mac from the [latest release](https://github.com/RechoRaccoon/Stellar/releases):
   - **Stellar-macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg** for M1/M2/M3/M4 Macs
   - **Stellar-macOS-Intel.dmg** for Intel Macs
   - (Not sure? Apple menu → **About This Mac**: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple silicon.)
- Open the .dmg and drag **Stellar** into **Applications**.
- The first time, **right-click** Stellar in Applications → **Open** → **Open** (the app isn't notarized by Apple). If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Updating:** download the newest .dmg and drag **Stellar** into **Applications** again, choosing **Replace**. Your sign-in and settings are kept.

# Using Stellar on a computer
- Stellar opens in a phone-shaped (portrait) window, since it was made for phones — drag its edges to make it any size or shape you like, and it remembers.
- **Esc** or the mouse's back button goes back.
- Sideways rows (like Mutuals and the profile tabs) scroll with the mouse wheel, or by clicking and dragging.
- VRM mode, the Camera page, AI Tagging, Translate, the audio visualizer and music scrobbling are phone-only, and show as "Not on Desktop".
- Something not working? Stellar keeps a log of errors: on Windows, paste `%APPDATA%\Stellar` into File Explorer's address bar; on a Mac, it's in `~/Library/Application Support/Stellar`. Send **stellar.log** along with your report.

# Install on iOS
- Download Stellar.ipa from the [latest release](https://github.com/RechoRaccoon/Stellar/releases)
- On Windows, install **iTunes** and **iCloud** from **apple.com** (not the Microsoft Store) (Mac can skip this)
- Then Install **AltServer** from **AltStore.io**.
- Plug your iPhone into your PC and click **Trust**.
- Click the AltServer icon on your tray → **Install AltStore** → pick your iPhone → sign in with an Apple ID (a spare one is safest).
- On iPhone:
   - Settings → Privacy & Security → turn on **Developer Mode** (it restarts).
- Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → **Trust** your Apple ID.
6. Open **AltStore** → **My Apps** → **+** → pick the Stellar file.

- Note: The developer mode toggle won't appear until AltStore is downloaded to your phone.
