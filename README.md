# Core Features
<img width="7424" height="2048" alt="Stellar screens banner v4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fe4318d-9d93-4548-b0bf-f647c60b27b4" />

- **The Hub** - Your customizable home page!! 
- **Profiles** - View your posts, blogs, reviews, music history, and more!!
- **Explore Mode** - View multiple posts at once from your favorite feeds!! It has multiple different layouts for different kinds of content!!
- **Timeline Mode** - View posts one at a time, with media fullscreened, and multiple interaction buttons available!!
- **VRM Mode** - Import your own VRM model into VRM mode, with face, hand, and full body tracking via your camera!! Take upload ready photos and videos, or even livestream to any platform straight from Stellar!!
- **Blogs** - View user's Standard.Site/Leaflet blogs, and even create your own!!
- **Reviews** - View, like, and comment on user's Popfeed reviews!!
- **DMs** - Create one on one or group DM chats!! You can send messages, share posts, profiles, and feeds!! There's even a streak counter, and special animations for holiday related messages!!
- (and so much more!!)

# Use Stellar in your browser
Open **https://rechoraccoon.github.io/Stellar/** — nothing to install. Works in Chrome or Edge 119+, Firefox 120+ and Safari 18.2+.
- Make the window any size or shape you like: a narrow window looks like the phone app.
- A wide window (or any device turned sideways) gets the landscape layout: grids fill the screen, comments sit beside the post, and DMs show your chats beside the open one.
- **Esc**, the mouse's back button, or the browser's back button goes back.
- Your sign-in and settings stay in that browser (clearing the site's data signs you out).
- VRM mode, the Camera page, AI Tagging, Translate, the audio visualizer, music scrobbling and saving as a GIF show as "Not on Web".

# Install on iOS
- Download Stellar.ipa from the [latest release](https://github.com/RechoRaccoon/Stellar/releases)
- On Windows, install **iTunes** and **iCloud** from **apple.com** (not the Microsoft Store) (Mac can skip this)
- Then Install **AltServer** from **AltStore.io**.
- Plug your iPhone into your PC and click **Trust**.
- Click the AltServer icon on your tray → **Install AltStore** → pick your iPhone → sign in with an Apple ID (a spare one is safest).
- On iPhone:
   - Settings → Privacy & Security → turn on **Developer Mode** (it restarts).
- Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → **Trust** your Apple ID.
6. Open **AltStore** → **My Apps** → **+** → pick the Stellar file.

- Note: The developer mode toggle won't appear until AltStore is downloaded to your phone.
